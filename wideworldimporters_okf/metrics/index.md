# Business & Operational Metrics

- [Net Sales Revenue](/metrics/net_sales_revenue.md): Pre-tax invoiced wholesale revenue excluding credit notes.
- [Gross Profit and Margin](/metrics/gross_profit_margin.md): Line-level gross profit and margin percentage across customer invoices.
- [Order Pick & Fill Rate](/metrics/order_fill_rate.md): Warehouse picking and order fulfillment accuracy ratio.
- [Accounts Receivable Outstanding Balance](/metrics/accounts_receivable_balance.md): Unsettled customer balances across accounts receivable ledger entries.
- [Inventory Reorder Exposure](/metrics/inventory_reorder_exposure.md): Stock items at or below reorder thresholds and target replenishment gaps.
- [Cold-Chain Temperature Compliance Rate](/metrics/cold_chain_compliance_rate.md): Percentage of cold room and refrigerated vehicle telemetry readings within safe temperature bounds.
